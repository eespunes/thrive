Delete your Thrive account

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Thrive account (package cat.eespunes.thrive) and its associated data. Thrive is operated by Erik Espuñes Jubero.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

The fastest way to delete your account is from within Thrive:

  • Open Thrive and make sure you are signed in.
  • Go to Account (your profile / settings).
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Confirm when prompted.

Your account and the data described below are deleted immediately. You may be asked to sign in again first to confirm it is really you.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email erik.espunyes7@gmail.com with the subject “Thrive account deletion request”.

  • Send from the email address registered to your Thrive account (or, for Google sign-in, your Google account email) so we can verify your identity.
  • State that you want your account and associated data deleted.

We verify that the request comes from the registered account holder before deleting any data, and we will confirm by email once it is done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your user profile record (users/{uid}): name, email, avatar, sign-in provider and saved app state.
  • Any family workspace where you are the sole member — including its budget content, its public join handle and its private join credential.
  • Your Firebase Authentication record (your login identity).

What is retained

DataWhy / how long
Shared family workspaces you do not solely own Kept for the remaining members. Your personal membership is removed, but the shared budget the household still uses is not deleted.
Security / backup logs May persist for up to 90 days for fraud prevention, security and legal compliance, then deleted.
On-device data The local cache on your phone is cleared when you uninstall the app.
Timing: in-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are actioned within 30 days of identity verification, and usually much sooner.
