10 ways to rework the 5-tab bar

Today: Home · Calendar · Lists · Finance · More, plus a floating + that changes meaning per tab. The usability issues: "More" is a junk drawer, five targets are small, the + overlaps content, and Finance hides a second switcher in its header. Each option below attacks one of those.

1aKeep 5, fix ergonomics

Smallest change: taller bar, bigger targets, pill behind the active icon, always-on labels, badges for what needs you.

1b4 tabs + centre Add

The floating + moves into the bar as a docked centre button (quick-add sheet). "More" becomes the avatar in the header — settings live behind your face.
